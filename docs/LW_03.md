## Implementation in this working tree

- `homeLayout.ts` defines a connected, obstacle-checked graph for the living room, work area, kitchen/dining area and bedroom. `scene.ts` renders the matching compact home with one character anchor, warm materials, practical furniture and a monitor whose colour follows a typed activity snapshot.
- `worldActivity.ts` maps the authoritative CA-01 task phase and actual Learning promise to `idle`, `awaiting_approval`, `coding` or `learning`. The renderer only reads these states; it cannot approve or execute a task. Approval waiting does not play the desk-work clip. Coding takes priority over Learning if both are active.
- `CharacterController` uses the same single rig and waypoint graph. Active work interrupts idle decisions, walks to the desk, sits and plays `desk_work` where available. Sofa sitting uses `sofa_relax` where available. The optional `stretch` clip is a bounded idle one-shot. Cancelling during a walk stops the route without teleporting. The Centre control cannot interrupt active work.
- `homeCamera.ts` supplies overview, follow, desk and sofa framing; the viewport interpolates position, look target and field of view. Home, Follow and Reset camera controls are in the existing panel. The 30 fps cap, hidden/minimised pause, reduced-motion pause and asset fallback remain in the same viewport.
- `create_astra_rigged_v2.py` reuses the V1 armature/export pipeline and authors separate geometry: brunette layered high ponytail, black relaxed hoodie without the V1 emblem or pocket, purple shorts, bare legs and black ankle boots. It exports a separate editable `.blend` and local `.glb`; V1 remains unchanged and is the default. The V2 selector is labelled **preview**, is disabled during active work and loads only through the existing allowlisted asset IPC. No external texture or model is fetched.

### Real defects found and fixed

1. **Stale coding approval control (product bug, CA-01 regression).** The renderer's 900 ms coding-state poll could overlap, and a slower response could land after a newer one, leaving the chat approval control showing the *previous* task's id. Approval then timed out in the CA-01 acceptance. The poll now refuses to overlap and ignores any response that is not the newest applied one.
2. **Occluded windows read as failures (harness).** On a busy desktop the Astra window is repeatedly occluded; the app pauses rendering by design, but the native scripts treated `worldState: "hidden"` as a crash or a stuck character. All four native scripts now launch with `--disable-backgrounding-occluded-windows --disable-renderer-backgrounding`, and the desktop verifier re-focuses the window and continues rather than failing.
3. **Chat approval timeout** (recorded in the previous session, still fixed): 10 s → 45 s with last-observed-state diagnostics.
4. **Centre assertion** corrected to accept any settled, non-transit state, and the **desk-work deadline** raised from 12 s to 40 s, because a walk across the room plus sitting down exceeds 12 s.
5. **Movement bound** in the idle probe now compares ground speed against the real sample gap instead of a fixed 0.45 m allowance.
6. **Disposable profile cleanup** retries and then warns instead of turning a passing run into a non-zero exit.
7. **Character geometry defects**, both real and corrected during the reference pass: the bare leg cylinder poked through a narrower boot rim as a sawtooth, and the fringe/hairline produced a visible seam. Both were fixed in geometry, not by loosening a check.
